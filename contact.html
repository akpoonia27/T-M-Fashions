The Boutique

TM Fashions

TM Fashions Design began with a love for hand embroidery and custom tailoring. Today we dress women across India — for weddings, festivities and the everyday elegant — with every piece still cut and stitched by hand.

We believe a garment should fit the woman, not the other way around. That's why every order is made to your measurements, with your choice of fabric, colour and embroidery — no two pieces are ever quite alike.
